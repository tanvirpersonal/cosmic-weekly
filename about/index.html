About

Curious science, told slowly.

Cosmic Weekly is a small magazine for people who like to understand how things work, and who would rather read one good story than scroll a hundred headlines.

Every week we publish a few stories about the universe and the people trying to make sense of it.

We write about black holes and snowflakes, about how machines learn and why we dream. The subjects change, but the approach stays the same: explain the idea clearly, say what is still unknown, and trust the reader's curiosity.

Each story is short enough to finish in one sitting and careful enough to be worth remembering.

Honest about uncertainty

Made to be read slowly
